About SIFIS

Designed to unlock smarter agriculture through AI.

SIFIS brings together environmental sensing, intelligent crop insight, disease detection, and farming automation in one modern experience.

Mission

To help farmers make faster, smarter, and more sustainable decisions with AI-powered intelligence.

Vision

To create a future where every farm can access intelligent tools that improve yield, reduce waste, and protect crops.

Objectives

Build a student-friendly yet premium interface showcasing sensor monitoring, forecasting, disease intelligence, and crop recommendations.

Problem Statement

Traditional farming often relies on manual observation and limited data, leading to delayed decisions and preventable losses.

Solution

SIFIS transforms the farming workflow through a data-first, AI-enhanced dashboard and smart operations interface.

Technology Used

HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, JavaScript, Chart.js, Leaflet.js, Font Awesome, AOS, and Google Fonts.

Workflow

Project workflow at a glance

1. Collect Data

Gather sensor and weather input from the farm environment.

2. Analyze Signals

Create insights from temperature, moisture, and crop health trends.

3. Predict Actions

Recommend irrigation, crop selection, and disease prevention.

4. Present Results

Deliver everything through an elegant smart dashboard.
